Phone & tablet

Turn the wheel at the bottom left with one finger. The pedals are analog: press near the top for full throttle or braking, lower down for less. Hold BRAKE when stopped to reverse. Drag on the road view to look around. Buttons light up when a function is on. Make the controls bigger in Settings → Touch controls size.

Gamepad

Left stick steers, RT / LT are throttle and brake, A = Enter, B = parking brake, X = engine, Y = camera, LB / RB = gears, D-pad = lights, horn and indicators, right stick looks around. Steering wheels that report as a standard gamepad work the same way.

First job
  1. Press E and wait for the air pressure to pass 5.2 bar, then release the parking brake with Space.
  2. Open the freight market with J. The GPS leads you to the trailer (green beam).
  3. Reverse slowly under the trailer until the prompt says it is lined up, then press T.
  4. Drive to the destination and stop in the yard. Reverse into the orange bay for a bonus, then press Enter.
  5. Keep to the limits: speed cameras fine you, and so does crashing into other drivers.
